Revival · Vol. I

Carrickfergus had an Arsenal club once. We're bringing it back.

A note from the founders · Carrickfergus · Season 2026/27

Years ago, this town had its own recognised Arsenal supporters' club, run by a local Gooner named Fred. Somewhere along the line it quietly wound down — and Carrickfergus has been without a home for its Arsenal supporters ever since.

That changes now. The Carrickfergus Arsenal Supporters' Club is being re-formed for anyone in the town and the surrounding area who follows the Gunners — a proper, friendly club built around the match day.

The plan is straightforward. We gather at a licensed local clubhouse for every game, on the big screen, in good company. We build a real membership. And in time, we apply to Arsenal FC to have the club officially recognised once more — picking up the thread that was dropped.

No corporate polish, no promises we can't keep. Just the football, the craic, and the colours — back where they belong.

Our History

The Original Club
Fred establishes the original Carrickfergus Arsenal Supporters' Club, gathering local Gooners together for match days.

Glory Years
The club thrives during the Invincibles era. Regular match viewings, away trips to the Emirates, and a growing community.

Quiet Decline
The club quietly winds down over the years. Carrickfergus is left without an official home for its Arsenal supporters.
